ABOUT THE LABEL

IMA Records is an independent record label and publishing company based in Los Angeles, operated by YAQI LLC. The label was built on a simple principle: the artist owns the work — masters and publishing stay with the creator.

IMA Records develops, releases, and licenses pop-rap and hip hop built for both streaming and screen, with full in-house publishing administration through YAQI LLC.

SYNC & LICENSING

The IMA Records catalog is available for licensing in film, television, advertising, and games. Masters and publishing are controlled in-house, so clearances move fast — one conversation covers both sides.
